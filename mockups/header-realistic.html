Header backgrounds · realistic, and light

Six ways to make V2’s header feel like a real place instead of a cartoon, each behind the same copy of the header. They follow what real apps do: Apple Weather paints a real sky and nothing else, Calm and Headspace keep nature soft and pale, and iOS blurs a photograph into a wash of colour. All six stay light, so the dark ink text stays as it is.

Photographs: “Kaziranga grassland” (the silk cotton tree), “Misty hills and valley reservoir at sunrise from Kolukkumalai, Kerala” and “Golden Meadows!”, all from Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 4.0. They are stand-ins to judge the look. A shipped header would use photographs ANTZ owns or licenses, ideally of the zoo’s own grounds.
