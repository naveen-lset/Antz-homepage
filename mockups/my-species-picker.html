My Species: a clearer way to pick

Five live sheets on the app's own data: 22 species across four sites, with five already on the home. Tap, filter, remove and press Done in each one. Every concept fixes the same five problems in the current sheet, and each takes a different route.

  • Thirteen chips on two rowsSite, status and class all take the same pill shape, so the filters are the loudest thing in the sheet.
  • Selected looks like unselectedThe only difference is a 40 px tick at the far edge. The row itself doesn't change.
  • Your picks are hiddenTo see what's on your home you have to turn on the "Selected" filter, which hides everything else.
  • Meta is cut off"Mammal · 9 animals · Bannergh…" repeats the class filter and still loses the site.
  • No order, no undoThe home rail has an order, but you can't set it here, and a mistaken tap can't be undone.
5 V5 · A's filters + B's photos

Pick by picture, filter in one row Build this

A's filter bar sits on B's photo grid. Search and the site dropdown share one field, and class plus New share one row whose counts follow the site. Under All, the grid is split into class sections. Choose a class and only that section shows. A picked tile gets a ring and its home-rail number in the primary green, #37BD69.

  • Tap a tileA primary ring appears, the + becomes its place on the home (1–n), and a face joins the foot.
  • Tap againThe tile is removed, later tiles renumber, and a toast offers Undo for 4 s.
  • SiteThe dropdown filters the grid and recounts every class chip. Zoo Core reads All 10 · Mammals 3 · Birds 4.
  • Class / NewA class shows one section; tap it again for All. New combines with either.
  • DoneCloses the sheet. The foot keeps “5 on your home” and the faces in view.
A Selection tray

Your picks sit on top; everything else is one filter row

A mint tray above the list shows what is on your home now, in rail order. Site moves into the search field as one dropdown, and class plus New fit on a single row. A picked row turns green and its button says “Added”, not just a tick.

  • Tap a rowIt turns green, the button changes to “Added”, and a pill appears at the end of the tray.
  • RemoveTap × on a pill or tap the row again. A toast shows “Removed · Undo” for 4 s.
  • ReorderDrag a pill in the tray. The number is its place on the home rail.
  • FilterThe site dropdown also recounts the class row. Filters never hide the tray.
  • DoneCloses the sheet. Changes save as you go, as they do today.
B Pick by picture

A photo grid grouped by class, numbered as you pick

Keepers know these animals by sight, so the photo is the target. Class chips become sticky section heads such as “Mammals 13 · 3 picked”. A picked tile gets a green ring and its home-rail number, and the foot keeps the faces of your picks in view.

  • Tap a tileA green ring appears, the badge changes from + to its order number (1–n), and a face joins the foot.
  • Tap againThe tile is removed, the tiles after it renumber, and Undo appears.
  • FilterSite segments and search sit above the grid. Empty class sections disappear.
  • DoneThe foot reads “5 on your home” with their faces, and Done sits beside it.
C Split by state

“On your home” and “Add more” are two separate lists

The state is where a row sits, not an icon on it. The top list is your home rail, which you can reorder and remove from. The bottom list holds only species you haven't picked, and the filters apply there alone. A species is never in both lists.

  • + AddThe row leaves “Add more” and lands at the end of “On your home”, where it flashes green. A toast reads “Added at #6”.
  • RemoveThe row goes back to “Add more” in A–Z order, and Undo appears.
  • ReorderDrag the ⋮⋮ grip. The numbers are the rail order.
  • FilterSearch, site and class apply only to “Add more”. Your picks stay in view.
D Dense checklist

One-line rows with a checkbox; filters fold into one button

This one is for curators handling dozens of species. Each row fits on one line, with its facts in columns so nothing gets cut off. An A–Z index sits at the edge. All filters live behind a single Filters button, and the active ones show as tokens you can remove.

  • Tap a rowThe checkbox fills and the row gets a green wash. The foot recounts.
  • FiltersOpens a panel with sites (multi-select), class and “Newly added”. The button reads “Show 12 species”.
  • Only mineA switch, not a filter chip, so it's clear it's a view of your picks.
  • Bulk“Select all shown” adds everything the filters leave visible. “Clear” empties the home, with Undo.
