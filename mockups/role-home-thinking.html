Role-based home: the thinking

The first mockup put made-up widgets on each role. This page works each role out from evidence: who the users are, what their day involves, what they decide, and what data ANTZ already holds. Each card in the mockup links back to a line here.

3 · What the real data says the homes must handle

1A death cluster that no total would show.At Crimson Frosted, one reptile species (Ochre erroralis) had 72 deaths in 10 days, and 20 of that species arrived in the 30 days before. That pattern points to disease brought in with new animals. A tile saying "157 deaths" hides it. The Vet and Biologist homes need a cluster alert: deaths grouped by species and site, measured against recent arrivals. The spec already names this medical.diseaseSurveillance.
2A backlog is not a to-do.14,296 deaths have no post-mortem report, and 3,475 of them are one species at one site. No vet can act on 14,296. So split it: "deaths in the last 7 days without a post-mortem" goes on the Vet's home, because it is today's work and the rules require a post-mortem for every death. The backlog belongs to records and compliance, on the Biologist and Admin homes.
3Records gaps block the annual inventory.28,532 animals have no sex recorded and 20,302 are not microchipped. Most are reptiles and birds, which usually need a DNA test to sex. Form II, the annual inventory to CZA, counts every species by male, female and unknown. These gaps are the Biologist's main records job, so they go on that home with progress toward the deadline.
4Sites are different kinds of place.Hollowpine took in 605 animals in 30 days (mostly snake rescues), while Clearspring took in 2. A home that only adds up all sites misses that. Every number must say which site it covers. The site chips already allow this; each card should honour them.
5Some of the spec's default numbers don't help anyone decide.The spec opens the Vet's home with "Total animals: 1,04,382". No vet changes their day because of that number. Its gauge, the "Facility health score", is the kind of single score the owner's brief rules out. So we keep the spec's sources but choose numbers by the rule below.

4 · The rule for what goes on a home

A thing goes on the home only if it changes what this person does today. Anything else lives one tap away, inside its module.

This comes from the product's own principle ("one glance, then one tap; the home stays light"). Each role is worked out with the same five questions:

  1. Job: what the person is responsible for.
  2. Day: what they do in the morning, during the day and at the end of the day.
  3. Decisions: what they choose to do today.
  4. Information: what they need to know to make those choices, and where ANTZ already holds it.
  5. Actions: where one tap should take them.

The same skeleton for every role

The layout is the live web home's. Only the contents change, so a person who switches role isn't lost:

Needs youA ranked list of the 3–6 things this person must act on today. Each says why and has one action.
PendingThis role's queues as counts. The coral panel already on the home.
Working viewThe one picture this role works from all day.
DeadlinesStatutory and internal clocks this role answers for.
Your modulesOrdered by the spec's priority list for the role.
AnnouncementsShared by everyone, unchanged.

5 · Veterinarian "Who needs me clinically today?" · 15 users

JobDiagnose and treat; run the hospital, isolation and quarantine; do post-mortems; set the vaccination and deworming schedules (RZR §5–7).
DayMorning: keeper reports, then the ward round. Midday: procedures and post-mortems. Afternoon: lab results, follow-ups, records.
DecisionsWhom to see first. Change a treatment? Isolate? Discharge? Cause of death. Is something spreading?
ActionsOpen the case, write the treatment, start the post-mortem, request a lab test.

So the home shows

  • Needs you, ranked: cluster alerts (finding 1) · critical cases · animals losing weight fast (the weighing round already flags the lion down 9% and the leopard down 15%) · new keeper flags · deaths today awaiting a post-mortem · lab results back. Spec: medical.criticalCases, medical.diseaseSurveillance, medical.labPending · app: care.js, assessment.js, necropsy.js
  • Working view: the ward. Every animal in care, grouped by where it is (hospital, under medication, isolation, maternity), serious cases first. app: care.js · spec: medical.isolationCases
  • Pending: Vaccination 143 · Deworming 319 · Post-mortems this week · Lab results · Follow-ups · Isolation. V5 database pending counts
  • Deadlines: the 24-hour death report for Schedule I species (the peafowl that died today is one) · the vaccination and deworming rounds.
  • Modules: Medical, Hospital, Lab, Pharmacy, Mortality, Follow Up. Spec priority: medical, pharmacy, animal-profile, alerts

Not on this home: total animals, species counts, the backlog of 14,296 (finding 2).

6 · Para-vet ("Doctor") "What has the vet asked me to do today?"

JobCarry out the vet's plan: give doses, dress wounds, collect samples, run the vaccination, deworming and weighing rounds, dispense medicine.
DayWorks through a list of timed tasks. The day is a timetable, not a dashboard.
DecisionsWhat to do next, and what is late. When to tell the vet a dose was refused or skipped.
ActionsMark a dose given or skipped (with a reason), record a weight, log a sample.

So the home shows

  • Needs you: tasks assigned to me, overdue first. Example: "Deworming round, Deer Paddock", 3 days overdue, from the vet. app: tasks.js
  • Working view: today's treatment board. Animals down the side, hours across the top; each dose shows given, late, skipped, due now or later. This is how vet hospitals run their whiteboards. spec: medical module; research: Vet Radar whiteboards
  • Rounds: the deworming, vaccination and weighing rounds by enclosure, with progress. Weighing is due every 30 days. V5 pending · app: assessment.js
  • Modules: Medical, Pharmacy, Lab, Diet & Kitchen, Follow Up, Hospital. Spec priority: medical, pharmacy, diet, animal-collection, welfare

Corrected from the first mockup: "Administer" is the admin approvals queue, not giving medicine, so it isn't on this home.

7 · Zoo Biologist "Is the collection record right, and is the population healthy?" · 9 users

JobKeep the animal records (history cards, identification, births and deaths); keep studbooks; plan breeding and avoid inbreeding; record behaviour and reproduction (RZR §9–10; CZA studbook guideline).
DayCheck the new events (births, deaths, arrivals, transfers) and correct the records. Plan pairings and exchanges. Prepare the statutory reports.
DecisionsIs this record right? Which animals still need sexing or a microchip? Which pairs to breed? Who to transfer? Is a sex ratio or group out of balance?
ActionsVerify a birth, record sex or a chip, plan a transfer, file with Parivesh.

So the home shows

  • Needs you: births to verify · a cluster of deaths seen from the population side (finding 1) · transfers waiting on a permit · animals kept alone too long (a mate within 6 months, RZR §9(4)) · Parivesh registrations due. spec: population.sexRatioFlags, welfareRisk.solitary, population.plannedTransfers · app: tasks.js
  • Working view: collection movement. Births, deaths, arrivals and transfers over 10 days for each site, and the species behind each. V5: 100 births · 291 deaths · 643 arrivals in 30 days · 16 transfers in a year
  • Records completeness: sexed and microchipped, as a percentage for each site, measured against the Form II deadline (finding 3). V5: 28,532 unsexed · 20,302 unchipped
  • Deadlines: quarterly report due 15 Oct (4 days) · Parivesh birth registrations · Form II due 30 Apr.
  • Modules: Species, Natality, Mortality, Eggs, Fetal Death, Parivesh, Reports. Spec priority: animal-collection, breeding, population, species-master

8 · Zoologist: offered as Welfare & Behaviour only if your zoos use it as a separate role

No source defines a Zoologist. If it's the same job as Biologist, merge it and use section 7. If it's a separate job, the one gap in the spec it fills is welfare: welfare-risk has no owner, and the rules require recording "social behaviour, group dynamics and reproductive biology" (RZR §10).

  • Needs you: behavioural concerns from keepers · welfare scores falling · animals kept alone long-term · elderly animals at high risk. spec: welfare.concernAlerts, welfareRisk.declining, welfareRisk.solitary, welfareRisk.geriatric
  • Working view: behaviour watch. Each flagged animal with its 7-day trend, from the keeper diary. spec: welfare.abnormalBehaviour, welfare.keeperDiary
  • Pending: welfare assessments due · enrichment response · corrective actions. spec: welfare.assessmentsDue, welfare.enrichmentCompletion, welfareRisk.correctiveActions

9 · Admin "What does the office need to approve, file or fix?"

JobApprovals, permits and statutory filings; user accounts and access; procurement and stock; staff compliance such as the annual zoonotic screening (RZR §5(9)).
DayClear the approval queue oldest-first. Chase filings before their deadlines. Handle access requests.
DecisionsApprove or send back. What to file before the next deadline. Who gets access to what.
ActionsApprove, review, file with Parivesh, unlock an account.

So the home shows

  • Needs you: approvals older than 3 days · permits about to lapse · filings due this week · locked accounts. live module: Administer · spec: compliance.expiryAlerts, compliance.pendingItems
  • Working view: the approvals queue, oldest first, filtered by type (transfers, purchases, access). Transfers need a permit (app: "Permit renewal, Barasingha transfer").
  • Compliance calendar: quarterly report, staff screening, annual report, and the post-mortem backlog as a records risk (finding 2).
  • Modules: Administer, Users, Security, Parivesh, Reports. Spec priority: alerts, compliance, finance, procurement

Left out on purpose: the spec's "Facility health score" gauge, because the brief rules out a single score.
