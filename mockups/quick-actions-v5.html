Quick Actions, four ways

Four live panels built on your reference, all 696 px wide like the app's panel. They use the same sixteen actions, four groups and five unfinished items that ?v=2 ships. Tap any action to see the next step: where it happens, with recent places filled in. Tap an unfinished item to resume it.

  • Kept from the referenceContinue sits above New, the four groups are tinted, and each action is a chip with its icon.
  • Changed in all fourThe status says who is blocking: Draft means you, Not sent means the network, Awaiting means someone else. The tap label matches: Resume, Retry or View.
  • What "useful" means hereA create has to land somewhere, so every panel asks where right away and offers the places you last used, before the form opens.
A Reference, cleaned

Continue on top, four tinted groups below Closest to the ref

This is your reference, tightened. Continue shows the top three and folds the rest behind “2 more”. Search sits in the New heading and narrows the chips as you type. Each group's heading opens its module.

  • Tap a chip“Where?” slides in with your three recent places. Pick one and press Start. If the action needs no place, such as New Site, it opens straight away.
  • Tap a rowResume, Retry or View, depending on who is blocking it.
  • TypeChips narrow and matches are highlighted. With no match, “Search everything for ‘x’” appears.
B One thing at a time

A sidebar of groups and a pane for the chosen one

This works like iPad Settings. The sidebar holds search, Continue with a count, and the four groups. The pane shows only the chosen one, with space for each action to say where it goes and where you last used it.

  • Opens onContinue whenever something is unfinished, otherwise the last group you used.
  • ContinueRows are grouped Draft, then Not sent, then Awaiting, each with its own button.
  • Action rowIt says whether it asks for an enclosure, a site or nothing, and shows your last use, such as “Last: CAR-03, 2 days ago”.
  • SearchTyping replaces the pane with matches from every group.
C Ready to go

Unfinished work as cards, then four suggestions with the place already set

This one guesses the next step. Continue becomes a row of cards with a progress bar. “Suggested now” offers four actions with the place already chosen and a reason for each. Every other action sits below as a plain four-column list.

  • SuggestionOne tap starts it. The place is already filled in, so there's no “Where?” step.
  • Change placeTap the place pill on a suggestion to open “Where?” instead.
  • All actionsA text list works like an index: tap a name and it asks where.
D Type what you want

A command field that understands the action and the animal

The field is focused when the panel opens. Before you type, it shows your four recent actions, what you haven't finished and every action by group. Type “leo” and it offers “Dispense Medicine · Indian Leopard, CAR-03” with the place already filled in, next to matching drafts.

  • Keys↑ ↓ to move, ↵ to start, esc to close. On touch, tap a row.
  • An animalMatches on species or enclosure code turn into actions ready to go at that place.
  • No match“Search everything for ‘x’” runs Global Search, as the app's field does today.
