Event landing page: every module with its own insight

A visitor scans a QR code or taps a link at the event and lands on one page that shows what ANTZ covers. Every module shows a live insight, so the page proves the product works without anyone needing to log in.

A visitor has no history in the app, so these parts would sit empty or make no sense.

4. Tapping a card

  • Modules already built (Species, Medical-Vaccination, Mortality, Necropsy, Eggs, Natality) open their full module page.
  • All other modules open a preview sheet: 3–4 insights, one chart, and “What this module does” in two lines.
  • Every screen has a back button, so a visitor never ends up on a page with no way back to the landing page.

5. Behaviour suited to an event (kiosk mode)

  • Nothing to edit: no +, pencil, swipe-to-add or Add Module.
  • Idle reset: after about 60s with no touch, the page goes back to the top and closes any open sheet.
  • Idle animation (optional): cards highlight one after another and the numbers tick up, to draw people to the stand.
  • Large touch targets for walk-up visitors.

6. Data and where it lives

  • Real, from the V5 dump: Collection, Species, Medical, Mortality, Necropsy, Natality.
  • Sample numbers: every other module. All of it sits in one event-insights.json, so the numbers stay consistent and are easy to change before the event.
  • A separate entry (?v=event or its own Vercel project), not a change to V2. It reuses V2's palette, cards, sheets and toggles without forking them.

7. Three layouts to choose from

RecommendedA. Module wallThe four family bands above. You see everything at a glance, which suits walk-up visitors.
B. Story scrollOne family per full screen, each with a big insight up top and its modules underneath. Good on a large screen or TV.
C. Zoo mapAn illustrated site map where tapping an area shows the modules working there. The most memorable, but the most work.

Questions before building

  1. Device: an iPad on a stand, a big TV, or visitors’ own phones via the QR code?
  2. How many modules: all ~20 listed above, or a featured 8–12?
  3. Sample data: fine for modules without real data, or should those cards show only “what it does” with no numbers?
  4. Layout: A, B or C, or a mockup of all three to compare?
