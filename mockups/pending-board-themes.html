Idea 8 kept: a note for each job, sized by how much is open. The cork is gone. The board is built from the same colours as the tiles around it. The four versions differ in how the card itself is set: the yellow paper it is now, a navy tile, a white card, or a mint pinboard. Each one is shown beside real V5 tiles, and each in its 2×1 size. Switch to 12 jobs to see it scroll: up and down on the 2×2, sideways on the 2×1.
