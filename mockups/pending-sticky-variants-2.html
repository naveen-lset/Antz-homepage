Pending, on yellow paper: six different approaches

The first set kept one list on one note and changed the question it answers first. This set changes what the note is: a pad you tear through one job at a time, a board of notes, the route you walk, a message from the last shift, a grid of urgency against effort, and the animals themselves. Each note shows the 2×2 and 2×1 sizes and says what a tap does. Taps work here, and in 7, 10 and 11 they act on the note itself. The first card is the current note, for comparison. Sample data: the open totals (the same seven jobs, 120 open) come from the database. The places, people, effort, ages and the animals named are made up.
