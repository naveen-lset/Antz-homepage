Quick Actions, five premises

The current panel tries to do two jobs in one scroll: finish what I started and start something new. It shows sixteen chips of equal weight in four colour groups, and the list is cut off, so it reads as a menu to study rather than a tool. Each idea below removes one of those assumptions. Every panel is live: tap anything and a note says what the app would do next.

  • What the review sawSearch, five drafts, four headings, sixteen chips and a footer, all at once. Nothing stands out, and the bottom row is faded half off screen.
  • What stays in every ideaThe same sixteen actions and five unfinished items from the app, V4's palette, the 696 panel, Search everything and Edit Modules at the foot.
  • How to compare themEach card lists the assumption it drops, what a tap does, where drafts go, and the cost or weak point.
1 Pinned tiles

Your six actions as big tiles, with drafts on the tileRecommended

Drops the idea thatall sixteen must be visible, and that drafts need their own list.

  • Opens onSix tiles the user picked, in Control Centre style. The other ten fold into one “All actions” row.
  • Tap a tileStarts a new record of that type.
  • Tap the badge“2 drafts” opens a small list of that action’s drafts, with “Start new” last.
  • PinThe pin on any row in All actions swaps it onto the grid. Edit Modules does the same for many at once.
  • AwaitingItems waiting on another person leave this panel and go to the Pending Actions card on Home, where they already belong.
  • Weak pointA new user sees defaults, not their own six. Ship role-based defaults (vet, keeper, admin).
2 Two tabs

Two jobs, two tabs: Start new or Pick up

Drops the idea thatone scrolling panel should hold both jobs.

  • Opens onPick up if anything is unfinished, otherwise Start new. The count on the tab shows what is waiting.
  • Start newA plain two-column list, no chips. Each row says what it will ask for (“asks: enclosure”).
  • Pick upGrouped by what you can do: Resume a draft, Retry an unsent one, or View one that is waiting on someone else.
  • SearchSearches both tabs and moves you to the tab with the match.
  • Weak pointA tab hides half of the panel. If people start new far more often than they resume, the default tab gets in the way.
3 Animal first

Start with the animal, then the action

Drops the idea thatyou pick the action first and the place later. Ten of the sixteen are about one animal or enclosure.

  • Opens on“Who is this about?” with the animals you worked on recently, plus scan and search.
  • Pick oneOnly the eight animal actions stay, already set to that animal. If a draft exists for it, a Resume bar sits on top.
  • Tap actionOpens the form with the site, section, enclosure and animal already filled in. It skips the three-level picker.
  • No animalRequest, Note, Announcement, User and the site actions sit in a small row at the bottom.
  • Weak pointIt needs a real animal index and recents. For admin users, who rarely pick an animal, it adds a step.
4 Folders

Four folders, each showing its two most-used actions

Drops the idea thateverything has to be on one level. You see four things, not twenty-five.

  • Opens onFour large category cards. Each one shows its two most-used actions as direct buttons.
  • Tap a buttonStarts that action right away. The common cases take one tap.
  • Tap a cardThe panel slides to that category’s full list, with a line of help under each action, and a Back button.
  • DraftsA single strip at the top, “5 unfinished”, which slides to the list. It stays one line tall.
  • Weak pointAn action outside the top two takes two taps. Search makes up for it, but only for people who type.
5 Step one inside

The panel becomes step one of the form

Drops the idea thatQuick Actions is just a launcher. Today the “quick” tap still leads to the slow part, which is picking the place.

  • Opens onA compact list on the left. The right side shows your unfinished work.
  • Tap actionThe right side turns into its first question: “Which animal?” or “Which site?”, with recent answers to pick from.
  • ContinueOpens the full form with that answer filled in, and a footer says what the form will ask next.
  • No-placeAnnouncement, Add User, New Site and Master Settings skip the question and open straight away.
  • Weak pointThe most work to build, since each form’s first step has to be defined. On a phone the two sides stack.

It also stops the separate draft list competing with the actions.
