Pending, on yellow paper: six new notes

The paper, tape, ink and ruling are the ones on the Home page now. What changes in each note is the question it answers first. Each note shows the 2×2 size and the 2×1 size, and says what a tap does. Taps work here: they show where the tap would go, and in 3 and 5 they act on the note itself. The first card is the current note, for comparison. Sample data: the overdue / today / this-week split in 2 and the “cleared” counts in 5 are made up. Only the open totals come from the database.
