Home header: four ideas from the Swiggy recording

Is it the same concept? Partly. The tabs that grow into the header, the rolling search hint and the wordmark with a photo that swaps all carry over well. The offer tiles don’t: we have nothing to sell. Their place goes to what the tab is about right now. Each version below keeps Swiggy’s layout and changes what the tabs mean. Tap the tabs, the wordmark and the tiles. Under each version: what every part does when tapped, and what it costs to build.

Kept · folder tabsThe selected tab is the same colour as the band, so tab and header read as one shape. Switching tabs recolours the whole band in 0.5s.
Kept · rolling hintOnly the quoted word rolls up, every 2.4s. It hints at what search can find: an animal name, a tag, an enclosure.
Kept · the wordmarkWORD [photo] WORD. With the same words, only the photo changes. With new words, the letters spread apart and close up again, as in the recording.
Changed · offer tilesThe tiles show where the tab’s work is: counts, groups, tasks. Every tile opens a page.
