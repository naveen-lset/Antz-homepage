## Acceptance checks (per shipped fix)

- **Review site:** open the link on a phone. You should see only V2, titled "Home Page Redesign · ANTZ", with no version rows in the profile drawer. If a version menu shows, an old build is cached; hard refresh.
- **Header and sites (Figma):** the top band is a still cream→mint gradient, and the site row is rounded chips with "All sites" in dark blue. If the header shows moving rain or sky, Background may be set to Climate in the drawer.
- **Note popup on a phone:** open any observation note. About 22px of the next card shows at the right edge, and on the first open the row leans toward it once. If there are no side cards, the phone is wider than 600px or an old build is cached.
- **My species / heads:** the favourites band reads "My species" with a pencil only. The Modules header shows a green + and pencil, with no words.
- **View list:** tap + under Modules. Tap a row and its module opens (Medical → its page; Hospital → an "opens in the ANTZ app" notice). Swipe a row left and "Add to Home" appears. Tap it and the row gets the house badge; tap ✓ and the card appears in Modules. If tapping adds instead of opening, an old build is cached.
- **Drawer:** open the avatar drawer. The rows are evenly spaced down to Logout with no dead gap, and nothing scrolls on a 667-tall phone.
- **Modules chip:** scroll down and tap the floating "Modules" chip. The Modules header, +, and pencil sit fully visible just below the pinned site chips. If the header is hidden behind the chips, the page is on the older build.
