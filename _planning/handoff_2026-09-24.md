# Handoff — 2026-09-24

If you (or a fresh session) opens this next, here's where we are.

The Quick Actions panel was rebuilt three times over 22–23 Sep and now draws
node **635:21410** on every version: a 696 white card with a search field, the
sixteen actions in four tinted categories as chips, and a foot row of *Search
everything* / *Scan a tag*. **`?v=2` alone** adds a contextual layer — a
three-row scrolling **Continue** band of unfinished work, a field that runs
Global Search's own index (`searchIndex.js`) ordered Quick Actions → Modules →
Continue → the rest with arrow-key travel, and a picker that asks where a
scoped create lands. V1/V3/V4 keep the flat panel and a chip-only filter, and
`verify_v2.py` asserts that absence on all three. Two deploys went out; the
live alias is byte-identical to the working copy.

**The single most important next move: commit.** Nothing from this session is
in git — production is ahead of the repo. `index.html`, `tools/verify_v2.py`
and **ten untracked `assets/icon/qa-*.svg` must land in the same commit**, or
the live chips render hollow the moment anything redeploys from a clean tree.

Two bugs this session came off the owner's iPad, not off a check — the panel
sat under the software keyboard, and the search field was being squeezed from
52px to 28px by `flex-shrink`. Both are fixed and now simulated in the suite.
The standing rule that came out of it: **run the suite against production, then
open it on the device the owner actually uses.**

**Pre-existing, not from this session:** `verify.py` fails 4 and
`verify_v2.py` fails 5 (version-menu radios, `checkDefaults`, a grid-hole
check) — identical at HEAD. And if `QA_CONTENT` is ever flipped back to
`'modules'`, four of the launcher's own checks are already red there: the
catalogue has grown to twenty modules and they still say nineteen.

## User-visible acceptance — what to try

Open `https://antz-module-selection-home.vercel.app/index.html?v=2` and tap
**Quick Actions**.

| Try this | You should see | If not |
|---|---|---|
| Open the panel on any version | A white card: search field, four tinted category groups of chips, and a foot of *Search everything* · *Scan a tag* | A flat 4×4 of grey cells means `QA_CONTENT` is back on `'verbs'`-as-list, or `.qa-chip` lost its CSS |
| Open it on `?v=2` | A **Continue** band above the chips, three rows tall, scrolling inside with a fade at the cut — not a "Show 2 more" button | A button means the disclosure came back; a four- or five-row box means `--qa-cont-n` or `--qa-item-h` drifted |
| Type `medic` on `?v=2` | Groups in this order: **Quick Actions → Modules → Continue** | Any other order means `QA_RESULT_ORDER` was re-sorted or the index's own `GROUP_ORDER` is being used raw |
| Type `leo` on `?v=2` | Animals, Species, Enclosures, Identifiers — a query that matches **no chip** still finds things | An empty panel means the field fell back to filtering chips; check `ctx` reached `runQuery` |
| Arrow-down through results | The highlight moves, the caret **stays in the field**, the next letter still types | If typing stops working, real focus moved into the list — it must stay `aria-activedescendant` |
| Tap **Dispense Medicine** on `?v=2` | The panel asks *where*, in place, sliding in from the right; second time it opens on the enclosure list with the path already walked and CAR-01 marked *Last used* | No question at all means the rejected binding row logic is back; a page change means it stopped being in-panel |
| Tap the field on a **real iPad** | The panel lifts to sit above the keyboard, whole list visible, field and foot both still on the card | Panel hidden behind the keys means the `visualViewport` listener is gone — CSS cannot see the iOS keyboard |
| Search with the keyboard up | The field stays its full **52px** | A squashed field means `flex: none` came off `.qa-search`; it shrinks to 28px |
| Open `?v=3` or `?v=4` and tap a chip | It fires straight out — no Continue band, no picker, no results list | Any of those appearing means `contextual` stopped reading `isV2()` |
